02 · Mode

Every white-filled area is copied to a new layer, filled and stroked 0.1 mm in spot “5”, overprint on.
Full-page spot “5” rectangle with the ticked spot colours cut out.
Click the preview to pick a colour. Only its visible area is cut from the spot “5” rectangle; anything painted on top stays filled.
Renders each page, finds every pixel with more than the threshold of colour, and pastes them as a 100% spot “5” bitmap layer above the artwork (overprint on).
Dither turns a fade into dots whose density follows the gradient, so it tails off to nothing instead of ending at a hard edge. Use a bigger dot size for plate-friendly dots.
Click the preview to pick colours; only those colours (and their fade to white) go into the mask. Nothing picked = every coloured pixel.

03 · Tools

Vector stroke sets the outline width on the new layer (0 = fill only). Raster spread grows the bitmap mask, or chokes it with a negative value. Visible-only cuts shapes off where another colour covers them. Invert fills the selection instead of cutting it out.
